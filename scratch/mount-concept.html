The stretch-loop idea, drawn

Plastic or metal, on and off in seconds, and small: the mount is one molded silicone loop and a nub, the mechanism bike lights have used for years. Nothing folds, clicks, ratchets, or can be lost.

1 · The whole mechanism: stretch, hook on the nub, done

The node is a small pebble with a silicone loop molded into one side and a nub on the other. Hold it against the bar or dumbbell head, stretch the loop over, drop it on the nub. Off is the reverse: thumb the loop off the nub and lift. That is the entire product — no lever, no buckle, no separate strap to lose.

2 · Why this meets your requirements — same gesture on everything

Magnets ask "is it steel?" — and plastic-shelled dumbbells answer no. Squeeze asks only "can I wrap around it?", and every barbell shaft, dumbbell head, and kettlebell horn says yes. One loop, three shapes:

3 · Where it goes on a barbell (unchanged)

The placement finding from the research still holds: clamp the smooth, non-rotating shaft just inside the collars — not the end cap, which spins with the sleeve and wrecks the gyro.

4 · Where it goes on a dumbbell

The band wraps the head — the weight lump — never the handle. Your hands live on the handle between the heads, so the node sits entirely outside the grip. It faces outward on the end, which also keeps it off your forearm, shoulder, and thigh.
